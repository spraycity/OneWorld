# ONEWORLD Community Code of Conduct

Our goal is a welcoming, safe, and respectful community across languages, cultures, identities, abilities, and levels of technical experience.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for people with less technical or language confidence.
- Respect dialects, accents, names, and language communities.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Protect privacy and obtain consent before sharing recordings, transcripts, or identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, intimidation, sexual harassment, sustained disruption, malicious impersonation, or sharing private information without consent.

## Reporting
Report concerns privately to the community moderation contact listed in the repository's current `CONTACT.md`. Until a staffed contact exists, open a private security advisory for security-sensitive matters or contact the repository maintainers through the hosting platform's private reporting mechanism. Do not post sensitive personal details in public issues.

Maintainers should respond fairly, confidentially, proportionately, and without retaliation. Serious incidents may result in warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.
