# Language Equity Policy — Draft

## Principles
- Language coverage is not measured only by number of languages listed.
- Publish tested language pairs, directionality, model version, and known limitations.
- Prioritize underserved and low-resource language communities through transparent criteria.
- Do not label machine output as human-verified.
- Respect dialects, scripts, regional varieties, and community naming preferences.
- Seek informed consent and appropriate rights for voice recordings and datasets.
- Compensate or credit community language work where funding allows; do not assume unpaid labor.
- Provide a process for communities to request correction, removal, or review of language data.
